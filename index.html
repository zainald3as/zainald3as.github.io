        CRYPTOGRAPHY · C

        Hybrid Caesar–Monoalphabetic Cipher

        Sep 2026 – now · CSCI 462 Introduction to Cryptography
        shift 3

        Six letters through a Caesar shift, then eight through a substitution table, then six again, and on. Each Caesar shift comes from the next letter, and the substitution table rotates after every three uses.

        My role: I developed the decryption algorithm. Each Caesar shift depends on a letter that was itself substituted, so the decryption first reverses that substitution to recover the exact shift. Every block is recovered without guessing.
